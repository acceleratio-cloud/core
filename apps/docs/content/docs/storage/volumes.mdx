---
title: Volumes
description: Persistent files for Docker services.
---

A Docker service can mount up to 10 volumes of 1–100 GiB each. Add them in
the service's **Configuration**, with:

- an absolute **path** inside the container;
- a **size**;
- a [storage profile](/storage/storage-profiles), fixed once the volume
  exists.

![The Add volume dialog: mount path /data, 5 GiB, storage profile Platform default](/screenshots/add-volume.png)

Volumes are part of the service's configuration: they are created, moved to a
new path and deleted by the next deploy. A rollback restores everything else
and leaves volumes as they are.

## One replica

A volume is attached to one server at a time. A service with volumes runs one
replica and is redeployed by stopping the old instance before starting the new
one, so each deploy and restart has a short pause.

## Size and usage

The size is the real size of the volume's file system. Volumes are thin: they
share the servers' free disk until written. A full volume answers writes with
"no space left on device", like any full disk.

The service card and Configuration show how full each volume is, refreshed
every minute. From 85% the card shows a warning, and from 95% the bar turns red.

## Not supported yet

Resizing a volume, and moving it to another storage profile.
