---
title: Users and roles
description: Invite people and choose what each of them can do.
---

**Users** in the top navigation has three tabs: **Members**, **Invite links**
and **Roles**. The owner and admins manage them; read-only users can view the
page; custom roles don't see it.

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including managing admins and transferring ownership. There is exactly one owner. |
| Admin | Everything except managing the owner and admins. |
| Read only | Sees every project and the cluster, without secrets. Changes nothing. |
| Custom | Only the projects its custom role grants. |

The first account, created at `/auth/installation`, is the owner. The
**Roles** tab shows the built-in roles with how many members hold each, and
the custom roles with their projects.

## Custom roles

A custom role grants each project one level:

- **Read**: status, logs, metrics and settings, without secrets.
- **Edit**: Read, plus changing and deploying services, revealing env
  variables and credentials, database exports and table rows, bucket
  downloads and the terminal.
- **Full**: Edit, plus deleting services and the project.

Create one with **Create role** on the **Roles** tab: a name, then a level or
**No access** for each project. Edit or delete it from the menu on its card. A
role assigned to members can't be deleted; give them another role first.
Deleting a role also deletes its open invite links.

Members with a custom role see only their projects. Cluster, Users and the
System and Integrations settings are hidden from them. They can deploy
marketplace apps into projects where they have Edit.

## Invite links

Choose **Invite people**, a role, and who can use the link:

- **One person**: the link creates one account.
- **Anyone with the link**: it creates accounts until it expires or is revoked.

A link is shown once, when it is created, and expires after 7 days. The
person opening it chooses their name, email and password. The owner invites
admins, read-only users and custom roles; admins invite read-only users and
custom roles.

The **Invite links** tab lists the links that still work: their role, who can
use them and how often they were used, when they expire and who created them.
**Revoke** stops a link at once; accounts it already created stay.

A link works only while its creator may still grant its role. Removing a
member, or giving them a role that can't grant it, ends their links.

## Members

The **Members** tab lists every account with its role and when it joined. The
menu on a row changes the role, removes the member or, for the owner,
transfers ownership:

- The owner manages admins, read-only users and custom roles.
- Admins manage read-only users and custom roles.
- Nobody changes or removes the owner; ownership moves only by transfer.

A new role, a removed member or an edited custom role applies on the next
request. Open terminals and live updates of that account close. Removing a
member deletes their account and ends every session.

**Transfer ownership** makes another member the owner. The former owner
becomes an admin, and the admin invite links they created stop working.

An account without a role (shown as **No access**) can't sign in. Give it a
role or remove it.
