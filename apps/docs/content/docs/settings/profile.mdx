description: Your account, password and sessions.
---

**Settings → Profile** shows your account.

## Password

Enter your current password, then choose a new one. Changing it ends every
session except the current one.

## Sessions

**Active sessions** lists where your account is signed in. End any you do not
recognize.

## First account

The first account is created once, at `/auth/installation` right after
installation. There are no seeded credentials to change.
