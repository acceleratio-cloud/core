## Logs

A project's **Logs** tab shows the output (stdout and stderr) of all its
services in one time-ordered list.

- Narrow it by service, instance, level and text.
- **Live** mode keeps following across deploys and restarts.
- Download the loaded lines.

![The Logs tab: filters by level, service and pod, a chart of lines over time, and worker log lines with warnings](/screenshots/logs.png)

Logs are kept 7 days or up to 2 GiB, whichever comes first, on the server's
own disk. A service that writes more than 2,000 lines per second is throttled.

## Metrics

A project's **Usage Metrics** tab shows, over a selected time range:

- CPU, memory, network and disk I/O per service;
- the fill level of volumes;
- for projects with databases: connections, transactions per second, cache hit
  ratio and database size.

Metrics are kept 14 days on the server's own disk.

![Usage Metrics: CPU, memory, disk, network and pods ready, with cluster saturation gauges](/screenshots/usage-metrics.png)

## Audit log

The **Audit Trail** tab lists who changed what in the project and its services, from
where, and when: deploys, setting changes, revealed secrets and opened
terminals.

![The Audit Trail: deploys, setting changes and a revealed environment, each with actor, IP address and result](/screenshots/audit-trail.png)
